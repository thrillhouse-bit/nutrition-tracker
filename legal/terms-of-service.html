2. Description of the service

Body Current is a personal-use tool for logging food and manual water intake, and planning daily nutrition around profile, goal, and training information you choose to provide. You can log food by scanning barcodes, photographing nutrition labels, searching, or entering it manually; record water intake; schedule training; and view daily energy and macro estimates, explanations, and progress. If you choose to connect a supported wearable (Oura, or Garmin after provider approval, or an Apple Health companion), the app can also use supported activity, recovery, and body-weight data in those views. The service is provided free of charge, as-is, and may change, break, or be discontinued at any time.
